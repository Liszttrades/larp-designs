Privacy notice

Your details, and what we do with them.

Short version: we only use what you send us to reply to you and quote your project. We don't sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

Last updated 15 November 2026

Who we are

LARP DESIGNS is a web design studio in Cavite, Philippines. We are responsible for the personal information you send us through this website. You can reach us at .

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us:

  • your name and business name,
  • your email address, or your phone number if you message us on WhatsApp or Messenger,
  • the package you picked and what you tell us about your project.

This website has no accounts, no payments and no database. The quote form doesn't send anything by itself. It opens your own email app or WhatsApp with the message filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

Why we use it

  • to reply to you and send a quote,
  • to plan and deliver your website if you hire us,
  • to keep a record of what we agreed.

We won't add you to a mailing list or send you ads.

Where it goes

Your message is stored in our email inbox, or in the WhatsApp or Messenger chat you used. Those services are run by Google, Meta and similar companies, and their own privacy policies apply to how they store messages. We don't share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad tracking. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads, as it does for any website that uses them.

If we add analytics or ad tracking later, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

How long we keep it

If you don't hire us, we delete your enquiry within 12 months. If you do, we keep project records for as long as the law requires us to keep business records, then delete them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop using it. Email and we'll reply within 15 days.

If you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
