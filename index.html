Questions before you book

Something else on your mind? Ask us on Messenger.

How long does a website take?

About 3 weeks for Starter, 5 for Business and 6–8 for Signature. The clock starts once we have your logo, photos and text.

What do I need to prepare?

Your logo, photos of your business or products, and the text for each page. No copy yet? We can write it with you for an extra fee.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. We register the domain and set up hosting in your name, so you own them. You pay those at cost.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. You get a short walkthrough at launch. If you'd rather not, the Care plan covers monthly edits.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. We keep what already works for your customers and rebuild the rest.

How do I pay?

Half to start and half before launch, by GCash or bank transfer. You get a receipt for both.
